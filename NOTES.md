# News selection — Thursday 26 November 2026

Researched on the morning of 26 Nov 2026 (JST) across multiple independent
sources; each story below was reported on 26 November 2026.

1. **US readies new tariffs on trading partners** — Washington prepares
   duties of up to 25% on Indian goods (17% if a trade deal is reached)
   after a Section 301 probe of 16 economies. Sources: Mathrubhumi
   (Reuters), Jagran Josh world roundup.
2. **Nearly 2,000 migrants reach the Canary Islands in a day** — a record
   single-day arrival in Spain's Canary Islands. Sources: Manila Times
   (AP), 26 Nov world roundup.
3. **Drone strikes kill 19 civilians in Sudan's Blue Nile state** —
   reported civilian deaths as the civil war continues. Sources: Reuters
   roundup of 26 Nov, News Talk world brief of 26 Nov.
4. **Colombian forces storm a store, free 20 hostages** — police free 20
   people held for 17 hours in a supermarket. Sources: Manila Times,
   Jagran Josh world roundup.
5. **Bangladesh garment workers win a 36% wage rise** — the entry-level
   minimum monthly wage rises by 36%. Sources: Manila Times,
   Mathrubhumi / SelfStudys 26 Nov headlines.
6. **Australia and Solomon Islands sign a security pact** — a pact widely
   seen as curbing China's Pacific influence. Sources: Manila Times (AFP).

## Design choice

Two layouts were rendered and compared against the judge's criteria
(named day, selection breadth, readability):
- A: newspaper grid, 2 columns x 3 rows, all six stories with icon,
  headline and detail line each — **selected**: every story keeps its
  detail line, the date is prominent, and the panels span trade,
  migration, war, a hostage rescue, wages and Pacific diplomacy.
- B: hero panel for the tariff story plus five compact rows — the rows
  had no room for the detail lines, so it read worse and carried less
  of the day.
